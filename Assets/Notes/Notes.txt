Lifecycle explanation:
Game starts
   ↓
Awake()       once
   ↓
OnEnable()    once, if enabled
   ↓
Start()       once
   ↓
Update()      frame 1
   ↓
Update()      frame 2
   ↓
Update()      frame 3
   ↓
... so if game is on 60 fps, that is 60 updates per second
